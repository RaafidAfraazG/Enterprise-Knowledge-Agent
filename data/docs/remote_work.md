# Remote work policy

Owner: People Operations
Effective: January 1, 2026. Company: Atlas Grove (fictional).

Eligible employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Teams agree on 2 shared office days every quarter. Customer-facing roles may require different attendance schedules, which must be recorded in the People Portal. Employees must remain available during core collaboration hours of 10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. in their contracted office time zone.

Request a regular remote work schedule using the FlexWork process at least 5 working days before it begins. The line manager reviews coverage, performance expectations, and workstation suitability. Approval lasts 6 months and must be renewed. Working from another country requires People Operations and Security approval 20 working days in advance; a domestic arrangement does not authorize international work.

Use a company-managed laptop, multifactor authentication, and the approved VPN for internal systems. Do not share devices with household members. Calls involving confidential information require a private room or headset. The company reimburses up to 40 currency units monthly for home connectivity with receipts. Report connectivity failures to IT Support immediately and agree on an alternative work location with your manager.

This fictional policy applies to Atlas Grove employees unless a local employment agreement provides a different entitlement. The policy owner reviews it each January. For an exception, submit a Policy Exception request in the People Portal with a business reason, proposed end date, and manager endorsement. Approval must be recorded before the exception begins; informal chat messages do not change policy. Questions go to the named service team through the internal help portal.
