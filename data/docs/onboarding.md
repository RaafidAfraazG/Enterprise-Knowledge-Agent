# Employee onboarding

Owner: People Operations
Effective: January 1, 2026. Company: Atlas Grove (fictional).

People Operations starts the WelcomeReady process 10 working days before a new employee joins. The hiring manager submits the role profile, equipment request, and first-week schedule. IT prepares a managed laptop and standard accounts at least 2 working days before the start date. No account may be shared with a new starter while access is pending.

On day one, employees attend a 90-minute orientation covering payroll, conduct, safety, and security. Complete identity checks and emergency contact information before accessing production systems. A named onboarding buddy meets the new starter twice in the first week. The manager explains team responsibilities and records a 30-day learning plan in the People Portal.

Mandatory privacy and security training must be completed within 5 working days. Role-specific access requires system owner approval and evidence of relevant training. Managers hold check-ins on days 7, 30, 60, and 90. People Operations collects feedback after the first month. Missing equipment or broken access should be reported through the New Starter ticket category, which has a 4-hour business-day response target. Completion of the onboarding checklist does not replace probation reviews.

This fictional policy applies to Atlas Grove employees unless a local employment agreement provides a different entitlement. The policy owner reviews it each January. For an exception, submit a Policy Exception request in the People Portal with a business reason, proposed end date, and manager endorsement. Approval must be recorded before the exception begins; informal chat messages do not change policy. Questions go to the named service team through the internal help portal.
