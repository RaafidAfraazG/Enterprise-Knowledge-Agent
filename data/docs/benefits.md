# Employee benefits

Owner: People Operations
Effective: January 1, 2026. Company: Atlas Grove (fictional).

Eligible permanent employees can enroll in the company health plan within 30 calendar days of their start date. Coverage begins on the first day of the following month after enrollment. Dependants can be added during enrollment or within 30 days of a qualifying life event. People Operations supplies plan summaries; detailed coverage and exclusions are governed by the insurer documents.

The learning allowance is 600 currency units per employee per calendar year. Obtain manager approval before purchasing courses, certifications, or professional memberships. Submit evidence of attendance and receipts through ExpenseTrack. Unused learning allowance does not carry forward. Mandatory role training is paid from the department budget and does not consume the personal learning allowance.

Employees may use up to 6 confidential counseling sessions per year through the Employee Assistance Program. Managers do not receive session details. The wellbeing allowance reimburses up to 200 currency units annually for approved fitness or ergonomic activities. Pension contributions follow the local employment contract and statutory requirements. Benefits eligibility for contractors differs and must be confirmed with People Operations. Review your nominated emergency contact and benefit beneficiaries every January and after significant personal changes.

This fictional policy applies to Atlas Grove employees unless a local employment agreement provides a different entitlement. The policy owner reviews it each January. For an exception, submit a Policy Exception request in the People Portal with a business reason, proposed end date, and manager endorsement. Approval must be recorded before the exception begins; informal chat messages do not change policy. Questions go to the named service team through the internal help portal.
