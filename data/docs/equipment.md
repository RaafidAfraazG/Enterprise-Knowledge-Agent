# Company equipment policy

Owner: Corporate Operations
Effective: January 1, 2026. Company: Atlas Grove (fictional).

The standard equipment package includes a managed laptop, charger, headset, and one external monitor. IT records every issued item in the Asset Register before delivery. Employees acknowledge receipt within 2 working days. Laptops are normally reviewed for replacement after 36 months; replacement is based on condition, security support, and role requirements rather than age alone.

Request additional equipment using the Equipment Request workflow. Items costing more than 250 currency units need budget owner approval. Ergonomic accessories require a workstation assessment and are usually supplied within 10 working days. Personal purchases are reimbursed only when preapproved in writing. Employees must not sell, lend, or transfer company equipment without IT authorization.

Report loss, theft, or damage to IT Support immediately and to Security within 1 hour when company data may be at risk. Do not attempt hardware repairs yourself. IT supplies return packaging for remote workers. Departing employees must return equipment within 5 working days of their final day. IT wipes returned storage using the approved disposal process and records completion in the Asset Register. Employees may not copy business records to personal devices before returning their laptop.

This fictional policy applies to Atlas Grove employees unless a local employment agreement provides a different entitlement. The policy owner reviews it each January. For an exception, submit a Policy Exception request in the People Portal with a business reason, proposed end date, and manager endorsement. Approval must be recorded before the exception begins; informal chat messages do not change policy. Questions go to the named service team through the internal help portal.
