# Annual leave policy

Owner: People Operations
Effective: January 1, 2026. Company: Atlas Grove (fictional).

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, plus local public holidays. Annual leave accrues monthly at 2.083 days. New starters receive a prorated allowance from their employment date. Part-time employees receive the equivalent allowance based on contracted hours. The annual leave year runs from January 1 to December 31.

Submit annual leave requests through the People Portal at least 10 working days before the first day away. Your line manager approves requests within 3 working days. For absences longer than 10 consecutive working days, obtain department head approval. Do not book nonrefundable travel until approval appears in the portal. People Operations resolves conflicting requests using coverage requirements and submission date.

Employees may carry over up to 5 unused annual leave days, which expire on March 31 of the following year. Sick leave does not reduce annual leave balances. Notify your manager before 9:30 a.m. on the first sick day; a medical certificate is required after 3 consecutive working days. Report an incorrect leave balance to People Operations within 30 days. Managers must arrange coverage without asking employees to work during approved annual leave.

This fictional policy applies to Atlas Grove employees unless a local employment agreement provides a different entitlement. The policy owner reviews it each January. For an exception, submit a Policy Exception request in the People Portal with a business reason, proposed end date, and manager endorsement. Approval must be recorded before the exception begins; informal chat messages do not change policy. Questions go to the named service team through the internal help portal.
