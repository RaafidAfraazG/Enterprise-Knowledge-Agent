# Expense reimbursement policy

Owner: Corporate Operations
Effective: January 1, 2026. Company: Atlas Grove (fictional).

Submit business expenses in ExpenseTrack within 30 calendar days of the transaction. Attach an itemized receipt, business purpose, currency, and cost center. Credit card statements alone are insufficient. Claims below 100 currency units need line manager approval; claims of 100 or more also need budget owner approval. Finance processes approved claims in the next payroll cycle.

Meals during approved business travel are reimbursed up to 45 currency units per day. Alcohol, minibar purchases, traffic fines, and personal entertainment are excluded. Shared meals must identify all attendees and the customer or project involved. Use the published monthly exchange rate for foreign currency claims and keep the original receipt. Duplicate claims are rejected automatically.

For a missing receipt, complete a Missing Evidence declaration within 7 days and obtain Finance approval. Claims older than 60 days require an explanation from the department head. Managers should review submitted expenses within 5 working days. Finance may request additional evidence and pauses reimbursement until that evidence is supplied. Suspected errors should be raised through the Finance Help queue, never corrected by submitting a second claim.

This fictional policy applies to Atlas Grove employees unless a local employment agreement provides a different entitlement. The policy owner reviews it each January. For an exception, submit a Policy Exception request in the People Portal with a business reason, proposed end date, and manager endorsement. Approval must be recorded before the exception begins; informal chat messages do not change policy. Questions go to the named service team through the internal help portal.
