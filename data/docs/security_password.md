# Security and password policy

Owner: Corporate Operations
Effective: January 1, 2026. Company: Atlas Grove (fictional).

Company passwords must contain at least 14 characters and must be unique to each service. Store passwords in the approved company password manager. Multifactor authentication is mandatory for email, source control, payroll, and remote access. Never send passwords or recovery codes over chat, email, or a support ticket. IT Support never asks for a full password.

Lock your screen whenever you leave your workstation. Managed laptops lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Report a lost security key within 1 hour through the Security Hotline. Temporary access codes expire after 15 minutes and may only be issued after identity verification. Shared accounts require a documented exception reviewed every 90 days.

Reset credentials immediately if you suspect compromise, then contact Security using the Account Recovery process. Do not approve unexpected multifactor prompts. Security reviews suspicious sign-ins within 4 business hours. Access to sensitive systems is reviewed quarterly by the system owner. Departing employees lose access at the end of their final working day, or earlier when required by People Operations. Personal devices cannot store unencrypted customer records.

This fictional policy applies to Atlas Grove employees unless a local employment agreement provides a different entitlement. The policy owner reviews it each January. For an exception, submit a Policy Exception request in the People Portal with a business reason, proposed end date, and manager endorsement. Approval must be recorded before the exception begins; informal chat messages do not change policy. Questions go to the named service team through the internal help portal.
