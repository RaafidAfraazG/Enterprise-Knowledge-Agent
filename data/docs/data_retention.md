# Data retention policy

Owner: Corporate Operations
Effective: January 1, 2026. Company: Atlas Grove (fictional).

Classify company information as public, internal, confidential, or restricted before storing or sharing it. Customer contracts are retained for 7 years after expiry. Finance invoices are retained for 7 years after the relevant financial year closes. Routine support tickets are retained for 24 months after closure, while access logs are retained for 90 days unless an investigation requires preservation.

Store records only in approved systems with role-based access. Do not create personal archives of customer data on laptops or removable media. System owners run monthly retention checks and record deletion jobs in the Retention Register. Backups follow a 35-day rolling schedule; deleted records may remain in protected backups until that cycle expires.

A legal hold overrides normal deletion schedules. Legal notifies system owners in writing and releases the hold explicitly when preservation is no longer required. Employees receiving a deletion request from a customer must forward it to the Privacy Desk within 1 working day. The Privacy team verifies identity and determines applicable obligations before deletion occurs. Report accidental disclosure within 1 hour through the Security Hotline. Retention exceptions need Privacy and Legal approval and must include a review date.

This fictional policy applies to Atlas Grove employees unless a local employment agreement provides a different entitlement. The policy owner reviews it each January. For an exception, submit a Policy Exception request in the People Portal with a business reason, proposed end date, and manager endorsement. Approval must be recorded before the exception begins; informal chat messages do not change policy. Questions go to the named service team through the internal help portal.
