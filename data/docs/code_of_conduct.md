# Code of conduct

Owner: People Operations
Effective: January 1, 2026. Company: Atlas Grove (fictional).

Employees must treat colleagues, customers, and suppliers with respect. Harassment, discrimination, bullying, retaliation, and deliberate exclusion are prohibited. This code applies in offices, remote meetings, conferences, company travel, and work-related messaging. Managers must intervene promptly when they observe unacceptable behavior and document the steps taken.

Declare actual or potential conflicts of interest in the Ethics Register within 5 working days of discovery. Gifts worth more than 50 currency units require advance Compliance approval. Cash gifts are never permitted. Hiring, purchasing, and contract decisions involving close relatives require an independent reviewer. Employees must not use confidential company information for personal financial advantage.

Concerns can be reported to a manager, People Operations, or the confidential SpeakUp channel. The Ethics team acknowledges reports within 2 working days and aims to provide a progress update within 10 working days. Reports are shared only with people who need the information to investigate. Good-faith reports are protected from retaliation even when an allegation is not substantiated. Deliberately false evidence may lead to disciplinary action. Preserve relevant messages without circulating allegations publicly.

This fictional policy applies to Atlas Grove employees unless a local employment agreement provides a different entitlement. The policy owner reviews it each January. For an exception, submit a Policy Exception request in the People Portal with a business reason, proposed end date, and manager endorsement. Approval must be recorded before the exception begins; informal chat messages do not change policy. Questions go to the named service team through the internal help portal.
