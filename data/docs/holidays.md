# Public holidays and office closures

Owner: People Operations
Effective: January 1, 2026. Company: Atlas Grove (fictional).

Employees observe public holidays for their contracted office location. London has 8 scheduled public holidays in the demo calendar, Bengaluru has 12, and Boston has 10. People Operations publishes the next calendar by November 15. A public holiday falling on a weekend is observed on the substitute day listed in that calendar, not automatically on a day chosen by an employee.

Public holidays do not reduce annual leave balances. Employees working part-time receive a prorated holiday allowance according to contracted hours. Teams with customer coverage obligations prepare a holiday rota at least 30 days in advance. A manager must approve holiday work before it occurs. Approved holiday work earns equivalent time off, to be taken within 60 calendar days.

The company normally closes offices from December 27 through December 30, subject to local operational notices. People Operations specifies by September 30 whether these days are additional paid closure or require annual leave in each location. Remote employees follow their contracted location rather than their temporary physical location. Employees transferring offices should ask People Operations to reconcile holiday balances before the transfer date. Raise calendar discrepancies through the Holiday Calendar ticket category.

This fictional policy applies to Atlas Grove employees unless a local employment agreement provides a different entitlement. The policy owner reviews it each January. For an exception, submit a Policy Exception request in the People Portal with a business reason, proposed end date, and manager endorsement. Approval must be recorded before the exception begins; informal chat messages do not change policy. Questions go to the named service team through the internal help portal.
