# Health and safety

Owner: Corporate Operations
Effective: January 1, 2026. Company: Atlas Grove (fictional).

Every employee must complete workplace safety induction within 5 working days of joining and refresh it annually. Office maps identify emergency exits, assembly points, first-aid kits, and trained wardens. Keep exits and fire doors clear. Report hazards through the SafeWork form on the same working day, even when nobody has been injured.

When the evacuation alarm sounds, leave by the nearest safe exit without using lifts. Proceed to the assembly point listed for your office and follow the warden's instructions. Do not re-enter until the site lead confirms it is safe. Fire drills occur twice each year. Employees needing evacuation assistance should agree on a personal plan with Facilities before attending the office.

Report work-related injuries to the manager and Facilities immediately; complete an incident record within 24 hours. For immediate medical danger, call local emergency services first. Remote workers should complete a workstation assessment every 12 months and after moving their desk. Take a short screen break at least once each hour. Facilities reviews reported hazards within 2 working days and prioritizes urgent risks immediately. Visitors must be signed in, receive safety guidance, and remain accompanied in restricted work areas.

This fictional policy applies to Atlas Grove employees unless a local employment agreement provides a different entitlement. The policy owner reviews it each January. For an exception, submit a Policy Exception request in the People Portal with a business reason, proposed end date, and manager endorsement. Approval must be recorded before the exception begins; informal chat messages do not change policy. Questions go to the named service team through the internal help portal.
