# Performance review process

Owner: People Operations
Effective: January 1, 2026. Company: Atlas Grove (fictional).

The company runs formal performance reviews twice each year, in June and December. Employees submit self-assessments by June 5 and December 5. Managers complete review conversations by the final working day of the review month. Reviews consider agreed goals, collaboration, role expectations, and evidence of learning. Ratings are not based solely on hours worked or office attendance.

Set 3 to 5 measurable goals in the GrowthCycle system at the beginning of each half-year. Managers and employees hold monthly progress check-ins and record material changes to priorities. Peer feedback should include specific examples and be requested from at least 3 collaborators. People Operations coordinates calibration to promote consistent standards across teams.

Employees receive the written review within 5 working days of the conversation. They may add comments or request a review of factual errors within 10 working days. Development plans should identify an owner, learning activity, and target date. Promotion proposals require department head approval and are considered separately from routine feedback. A performance concern must be discussed promptly rather than saved for the formal review. Employees on extended leave receive an adjusted timetable agreed with People Operations.

This fictional policy applies to Atlas Grove employees unless a local employment agreement provides a different entitlement. The policy owner reviews it each January. For an exception, submit a Policy Exception request in the People Portal with a business reason, proposed end date, and manager endorsement. Approval must be recorded before the exception begins; informal chat messages do not change policy. Questions go to the named service team through the internal help portal.
