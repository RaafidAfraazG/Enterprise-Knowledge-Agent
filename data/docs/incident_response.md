# Security incident response

Owner: Corporate Operations
Effective: January 1, 2026. Company: Atlas Grove (fictional).

Report suspected security incidents to the Security Hotline within 1 hour of discovery. Examples include lost devices, phishing, unexpected data exports, leaked credentials, and malware alerts. Use the IncidentReady process and include the discovery time, affected system, observed behavior, and a safe contact method. Do not investigate independently or delete evidence.

Security assigns an incident commander for severity 1 and severity 2 incidents. Severity 1 means active compromise of a critical service or confirmed exposure of restricted data; the acknowledgement target is 15 minutes. The commander opens a response channel, assigns containment tasks, and publishes an internal update every 30 minutes until the situation stabilizes. Only designated Communications and Legal representatives contact customers or regulators.

If instructed, disconnect the affected device from networks while leaving it powered on for evidence collection. Preserve original messages and timestamps. Recovery requires system owner approval and a documented check that containment measures remain effective. Security produces a preliminary incident report within 2 working days after containment. A blameless review occurs within 5 working days after recovery, with corrective actions assigned owners and deadlines. Confidential incident details must not be shared in public channels.

This fictional policy applies to Atlas Grove employees unless a local employment agreement provides a different entitlement. The policy owner reviews it each January. For an exception, submit a Policy Exception request in the People Portal with a business reason, proposed end date, and manager endorsement. Approval must be recorded before the exception begins; informal chat messages do not change policy. Questions go to the named service team through the internal help portal.
