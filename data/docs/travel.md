# Business travel policy

Owner: Corporate Operations
Effective: January 1, 2026. Company: Atlas Grove (fictional).

Book business travel through TravelDesk after manager approval. Domestic trips require approval 7 calendar days in advance; international trips require 21 days. Use economy class for flights shorter than 8 hours. Premium economy on longer flights requires department head approval. Personal upgrades are not reimbursed, and loyalty preferences must not increase the company fare.

The standard hotel cap is 150 currency units per night before local taxes. Finance publishes exceptions for high-cost cities quarterly. Choose accommodation near the work location where practical. Use public transport or licensed taxis; rental vehicles need a documented business reason. Keep booking confirmations and itemized receipts for expense claims. TravelDesk maintains a 24-hour assistance number for disrupted journeys.

Check passport validity, visas, required vaccinations, and destination guidance before booking. Security approval is mandatory for destinations marked elevated risk in the Travel Register. Share your itinerary and emergency contact details with TravelDesk 48 hours before departure. Report safety incidents immediately through the Security Hotline. Employees may decline travel when an unresolved medical or safety concern exists. Any personal days added to a business trip must be approved and paid for separately.

This fictional policy applies to Atlas Grove employees unless a local employment agreement provides a different entitlement. The policy owner reviews it each January. For an exception, submit a Policy Exception request in the People Portal with a business reason, proposed end date, and manager endorsement. Approval must be recorded before the exception begins; informal chat messages do not change policy. Questions go to the named service team through the internal help portal.
